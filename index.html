Privacy Policy — EyeDuo

Effective date: November 19, 2026 · App ID: com.norkatanya.lazyeye · Developer: Norka Tanya

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app EyeDuo (the “App”) for Android handles information. The App is a set of 3D games for anaglyph (red-cyan or red-green) glasses. In short: the App does not collect personal data and does not send it to us.

1. Information we do not collect

  • The App has no user accounts, registration or login, and no servers of its own.
  • The App does not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising ID.
  • The App contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

2. Data stored on your device

Profiles (a name, mode and icon), game history (time, levels, hits), glasses calibration and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting a profile, clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App deletes this information.

3. Camera and microphone

The App does not use the camera or the microphone.

4. In-app purchase

The App offers one optional one-time purchase (“Full version”). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or billing details. Google Play only confirms to the App that the purchase was made, so the full version can be unlocked and restored. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Other features

  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.
  • Own music file (full version): the audio file you choose is copied inside the App and is never uploaded.
  • Voice prompts use the text-to-speech engine installed on your device.

6. Device permissions

  • Notifications and run at startup — game reminders (optional).
  • Storage access on older Android versions — to choose your own music file (optional).
  • Network access and billing — required by Google Play to process the purchase.
  • Vibration — notification feedback.

7. Children

The App is designed for children and adults. Children should use it under adult supervision. Because we collect no personal data, we do not collect any information from children. In a kids profile, settings and the purchase are protected by a parent PIN, and the kids interface has no ads and no external links.

8. Disclaimer and limitation of liability

  1. EyeDuo is a set of games. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
  2. For a child, a parent or legal guardian accepts these terms and supervises the play.
  3. You use the App at your own risk. To the extent permitted by law, the developer is not liable for any harm to health, other consequences, or direct or indirect damage related to the use of the App.
  4. Take breaks. If you feel discomfort, headache, eye strain or double vision, stop playing. If it does not pass, see a doctor.
  5. Every user must accept these terms on the App's first screen before using it. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

9. Changes to this policy

If future versions of the App add features that process data, this policy will be updated on this page before such features are released, and the effective date above will change. If the terms of use change, the App asks every user to accept them again.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: norka.tanya@gmail.com

1. Какие данные мы не собираем

Профили (имя, режим, значок), история игр (время, уровни, попадания), калибровка очков и настройки хранятся только на вашем устройстве и никогда не отправляются нам. При удалении профиля, очистке данных или удалении Приложения эта информация удаляется.

Приложение не использует камеру и микрофон.

Google Play лишь сообщает Приложению, что покупка совершена, чтобы открыть и восстановить полную версию. Как Google обращается с вашими данными, описано в Политике конфиденциальности Google.

  • Голосовые подсказки используют синтезатор речи, установленный на вашем устройстве.
